# Methodology

## Research approach

The project collects and classifies publicly available information about India’s semiconductor ecosystem.

The research categories are:

1. Government programmes and policies.
2. Semiconductor manufacturing and backend projects.
3. DLI startups and fabless chip-design companies.
4. ISRO and DAE-linked institutions.
5. DRDO electronics and semiconductor laboratories.
6. Bengaluru/Karnataka ecosystem entities.
7. EDA and technical-sales opportunities.

## Record classification

Each record should contain:

- Organisation or project name.
- Organisation type.
- Location.
- Value-chain segment.
- Technical domain.
- Government-programme connection.
- Current status.
- Source URL.
- Publication date.
- Last verified date.
- Confidence level.

## Confidence levels

- High: Confirmed by an official government or company source.
- Medium: Reported by a credible publication and supported by additional evidence.
- Low: Mentioned in secondary sources and requiring verification.

## Opportunity scoring

EDA opportunity scores may consider:

- Technical fit.
- Project maturity.
- Funding or government support.
- Bengaluru/Karnataka presence.
- Likelihood of EDA adoption.
- Strategic importance and reference potential.

## Update frequency

Recommended update schedule:

- Policy tracker: monthly.
- Project status: monthly or quarterly.
- DLI startup records: quarterly.
- Bengaluru account mapping: quarterly.
- EDA opportunity scores: after every major company or policy update.
