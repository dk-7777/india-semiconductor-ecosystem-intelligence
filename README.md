# India Semiconductor Ecosystem Intelligence

An Excel-based semiconductor ecosystem intelligence project focused on India, with a detailed territory view of Bengaluru and Karnataka.

## Overview

This project maps India’s developing semiconductor ecosystem across government policy, chip design, semiconductor manufacturing, ATMP/OSAT, strategic electronics, research institutions, startups and EDA opportunities.

The project combines:

- An Excel-based organisation and policy database.
- Semiconductor project and investment tracking.
- DLI startup mapping.
- ISRO, DAE and DRDO electronics ecosystem mapping.
- Bengaluru/Karnataka territory analysis.
- EDA opportunity identification.
- A PowerPoint territory report.

## Objectives

The project aims to answer:

1. Which organisations are building India’s semiconductor ecosystem?
2. What do Semicon India, ISM, PLI, DLI and C2S enable?
3. Which fabs, ATMP and OSAT projects are being developed?
4. Which DLI startups are working on chip design and semiconductor IP?
5. How are ISRO, DAE and DRDO connected to electronics and semiconductor capabilities?
6. Why is Bengaluru important to India’s semiconductor design ecosystem?
7. Where are potential opportunities for EDA tools, training, partnerships and technical sales?

## Key concepts

- **Fab:** Semiconductor fabrication plant.
- **ATMP:** Assembly, Testing, Marking and Packaging.
- **OSAT:** Outsourced Semiconductor Assembly and Test.
- **DLI:** Design Linked Incentive scheme.
- **EDA:** Electronic Design Automation.
- **SoC:** System-on-Chip.
- **IP:** Reusable semiconductor intellectual property.
- **Tape-out:** Final submission of a chip design for manufacturing.

## Methodology

The database organises information by:

- Organisation type.
- Semiconductor value-chain segment.
- Geographic location.
- Project status.
- Technical domain.
- Government programme.
- Bengaluru/Karnataka relevance.
- Potential EDA requirement.
- Opportunity priority.

Each important record should be supported by a source URL and a last-verified date.

## Bengaluru/Karnataka focus

Bengaluru is treated primarily as a semiconductor design, EDA, research, aerospace, defence, embedded-systems and startup hub.

The territory analysis considers:

- Global semiconductor R&D centres.
- EDA companies.
- Fabless chip-design startups.
- Universities and research institutions.
- ISRO and DRDO-linked electronics work.
- Technical-sales and business-development opportunities.
- Karnataka’s electronics and semiconductor policy environment.

## Important note

Project status, investment values, production timelines, DLI participation and policy details can change. Information should be verified against official MeitY, ISM, company, ISRO, DRDO and government sources before being used for formal commercial, academic or investment decisions.
